Sheets Connectors

Free tools that put your Toggl Track time entries and your Calendly bookings into your own Google Sheet, and keep them up to date.

  • Toggl Track to Google Sheets: time entries with descriptions, projects, clients and tags.
  • Calendly to Google Sheets: one row per booking, with every booking question as its own column. Works on Calendly's free plan.

Both run inside your Google account as Google Apps Script. Your data goes straight from Toggl or Calendly into your spreadsheet. It never passes through a server of mine.
